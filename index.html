About me

I have been playing Minecraft since some time around 2010. I also enjoy programming and have been working on my own mods for the last couple of years as well as contributing to a few other mods.

I mostly focus on mods that are available for the latest version of Minecraft. It's always a little exciting to see which mods have updated to the latest release!

I usually play on Fabric, although I do occasionally use Neoforge as well. For my own mods, I try to make them available for both.

My Mods

Works in Progress

In addition to the mods that I have published and available, I have several more that are in various states of planning or implementation. Let me know if you want them sooner, and I may focus my efforts in that direction.

Oritech Souls

An addon for Oritech. Adds the ability to transfer souls through soul pipes, and adds integration with a few other mods that produce or use souls.

Bushcraft

A low-tech tech mod for the early game with a focus on making survival and gathering more fun.

Amethyst Lights

Lights that work similar to candles, but are made of glowing purple crystals. You can place up to 4 of them in a single block.

Odd Pipes

A standalone pipe mod similar to the pipes from Quark Oddities, but also has fluid, energy, and possibly soul pipes.

Featured Mods

These are some of the mods I love playing with. I tend to like mods that add new or interesting features, interact well with other mods, and don't get excessive with adding new blocks and items.
